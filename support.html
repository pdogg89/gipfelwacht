Summit Defender – Help

Tower Defense in the Alps · for iPhone

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to hello@minetgames.ch. We usually reply within a few days.

How to play

  • Tap a free spot and pick a tower at the bottom. Tap a tower to upgrade or sell it.
  • Start the wave with the green button. Starting early earns bonus gold.
  • The top left shows the weather for the current and the next wave. Tap it to see what it does.
  • Different towers side by side form a rope team: +10 % for each other kind, up to +30 %.
  • Enemies inside a tunnel are protected. The blast charge hits everyone in there.

FAQ

How do I get crystals? Only by playing: new stars, achievements, the Daily Expedition, the Hut Book and the Endless Climb. They only change how things look.

Will I lose my progress when the app updates? No. It stays on your iPhone until you delete the app or choose “Delete progress” in Settings.

Does the game need the internet? No, it runs fully offline.
