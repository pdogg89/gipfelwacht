Summit Defender Privacy Policy

App for iPhone · Last updated: November 9, 2026

In short: Summit Defender collects no data. There is no account, no analytics and no internet connection. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

Data controller

Philippe Minet, Switzerland
Email: minet.philippe@sunrise.ch

What data the app processes

No personal data. The app does not ask for your name, email address or location, and it does not access your camera, microphone, photos or contacts.

Progress on your device

Levels, stars, Workshop upgrades, crystals, achievements, the Hut Book and settings are stored only locally in the app. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else. They may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup, which Apple manages for you.

Choosing Settings › “Delete progress” in the app, or deleting the app, removes everything.

No third parties

This version contains no ad networks, no analytics or tracking services and no cookies. There is no tracking across apps or websites of other companies.

Beta versions via TestFlight

If you use a beta version through TestFlight, Apple provides the developer with crash reports, device information and any feedback you choose to send. Apple’s privacy policy applies to this.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you can ask what data is held about you. Since no data from the app ever reaches the developer, there is nothing to correct or delete. We are happy to answer questions by email.

Changes

If anything about how data is handled changes, for example through new features, we will update this policy beforehand and point it out in the app.
